## 1.0.0-rc1

* Initial release candidate.
* Device enrollment with an issuer on the Strivacity backend from an enrollment grant (QR code or deep link).
* Listing and un-enrolling enrolled devices.
* Receiving push-based sign-in challenges and resolving them (approve, deny or answer).
* Reporting rotated push tokens via `updateDeviceToken`.
* Android (API 29+) and iOS (15+, Swift Package Manager) support.
